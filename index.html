yt-pipeline

yt-pipeline is a private tool that its owner runs on their own computer to manage their own YouTube channels. It is not a public service: nobody else can sign in to it or use it.

What it does

It reads, with read-only access, the videos and the analytics of the owner's channels, including revenue reports. The owner uses these figures to plan and improve future videos. It never uploads, changes or deletes anything on YouTube.
