Privacy policy and terms of use

Last updated: November 2, 2026. Back to yt-pipeline.

YouTube API Services

yt-pipeline uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Who uses it

Only the owner of yt-pipeline uses it, with their own Google account and their own channels. It collects no data from any other person.

What it accesses

With read-only permission from the owner, it accesses channel and video information, YouTube Analytics reports and revenue reports for the owner's channels.

How the data is stored and used

The data and the access tokens are stored on the owner's own computer. The data is used only to help the owner plan and improve their videos.

Who the data is shared with

Excerpts of the data are sent to Claude, the AI service from Anthropic that the owner has approved, so it can analyze them on the owner's behalf. The data is not sold, and it is not shared with anyone else. Access tokens are never shared.

Advertising and cookies

yt-pipeline serves no advertising. It places no cookies or similar technology on anyone's device, and neither does this website.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page. The owner can also delete the stored data from their computer.

Contact

For questions or complaints, open an issue on this website's repository.
